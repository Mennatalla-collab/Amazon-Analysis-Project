Amazon Sales & Order Analysis

Project Overview

An Excel-based dashboard focused on analyzing e-commerce sales performance, customer purchasing trends, category growth, and regional order distribution.

Key Insights

- Electronics dominates overall revenue compared to other product categories.
- The United States accounts for the majority of total sales volume and order count.
- Credit Card is the primary payment method preferred by customers.

Tools Used

Excel | Pivot Tables | Pivot Charts | Interactive Slicers
